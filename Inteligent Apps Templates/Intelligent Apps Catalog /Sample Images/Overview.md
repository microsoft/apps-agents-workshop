**How to Use Sample Images**
============================

This repository includes sample images to help you understand how the **Generative Pages (Gen Pages)** experience works. However, when configuring your own **Use Cases** and **App Catalog**, you should replace the sample visuals with your organization’s actual images and icons.

**Gen Pages Sample Images**
---------------------------

This folder contains example images demonstrating how generative UX pages can look.Use these images **only as samples** to populate your **UX Catalog** when exploring or testing the tool.

When building your own catalog, you can continue using these sample Gen Page images—or replace them with your own generated UX screenshots if preferred.

**Use Case Sample Images**
--------------------------

These images are included as placeholders to illustrate how Use Cases appear in the catalog.When setting up your real **Use Cases Catalog**, make sure to replace these with **your own solution images**, such as:

*   product screenshots
    
*   architecture diagrams
    
*   feature showcase images
    
*   branding or solution-specific artwork
    

This ensures your Use Case repository accurately reflects your organization’s solutions.

**App Catalog Sample Images**
-----------------------------

The sample app icons provided in this folder are intended only as examples.When configuring the **App Catalog**, you should upload your own:

*   app icons
    
*   product logos
    
*   custom app imagery
    

This will create an App Hub experience that correctly represents the applications your organization hosts.
